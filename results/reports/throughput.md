## Caveats

- Clips may already be in the page cache (the same seeded clips as earlier runs); not controlled. RGB video is 1.9 GB per hour, IR 0.04 GB per hour.
- The CPU is a hybrid design (performance and efficiency cores) in a laptop: decode speed depends on which cores the OS uses and on temperature. See the run-to-run table.
- CLIP weights untrained: the weight tag (appearance.pretrained, TBD) does not change the compute.
- Preprocessing is the model's default: shortest side to 224, then a **centre crop to 224x224**, which cuts the left and right ~22 % of a 16:9 frame. Whether full frames should be padded or tiled instead is a step 4.2 decision. It ran in one process; splitting it over CPU workers was not measured.
- Not measured here: the detector (Gate 3.3), MOG2 itself, crop extraction and crop preprocessing, the action branch.
