(this file is the only output). Method: 8 independent read-only reviewers, one per dimension, each required to give
concrete evidence (file:line, command output, commit, or a recomputed number); then one skeptical verifier per dimension
re-checked every critical/major finding and confirmed, adjusted or refuted it (16 agents). Minor findings were not
independently verified (marked "unverified"). Claude spot-checked three findings directly (leakage-6, repro-1,

## Your three concerns, with the evidence

1. **"Object detection is kinda weak."** Partly, but retrieval doesn't use it at all. YOLOX-s finds 0.41 of RGB people and
   0.83 of vehicles, 0.24 of people under 5 % of frame height, about 0 in IR (detector_3.3.md). It did **not** pass
   Gate 3.3 by its rule (overall 0.414 / pooled 0.476 < 0.5): you kept it as a documented deviation. Detection crops
   then lost to motion crops at Gate 4.4 (via a post-hoc tie-break) and on dev at 8.1 (6/12 vs 9/12 found, but better
   ranks when found: R@10 0.417 vs 0.333), mainly because their extra vectors crowd the same 200-vector list. The
   delivered system searches **motion crops only, at most 4 per frame**, which cover **6 % of annotated people and 38 %
   of vehicles** (tuning; crops_4.2.md). The ≤4 detection-then-motion crops would cover 15 % / 51 %; all detections
   40 % / 81 %. So the bigger loss is crop *selection and budget*, then CLIP's resolution (weakness-1, weakness-3).
2. **"Outside still gives the gym."** Confirmed and explained (weakness-2, ui-1, F6): every vector competes in one
   raw-cosine top-200; crop cosines sit well above whole-frame cosines (dev 99th percentile 0.276 vs 0.221), so frames
   fill only 5.9 % of list slots and 9 of 12 dev specific queries get no frame vector at all; a frame is then scored
   by its best view (the crop). Scene words live in the frame vector, which ranking never uses when a crop wins. The
   UI's only remedy is manual camera exclusion. Fix direction: frames and crops as separate fusion branches (own
   top-k and z-score), or crop + w × same-frame score (dev only, exploratory, pre-registered).
3. **"Accuracy is below what the planned architecture should deliver."** The planned architecture was not delivered:
   detection crops unused, action branch at weight 0 (kept by an 8/1/8 count tie, F2), event branch dropped (rule
   P/R < 0.5, F1), centering rejected. What remains is appearance-only CLIP B/16 with motion crops. Measured causes of
   the 11 test misses: small/far objects (5), IR (3; IR is 1/4 found), brief action, ambiguous text, dedup hole
   (weakness-3, -4; failures.md, Claude's labels). Two evaluation factors make it look worse/noisier than it is:
   (a) a fixed 200-vector list (never tuned) with a full-review miss cost turns the median into a hit/miss coin flip
   — on dev, k=800 finds 11/12 and k=2000 12/12, trading median up for mean down (weakness-5); (b) every gate was
   decided on 6-12 specific queries, half of dev on the easy indoor camera G330 (found 6/6 dev, 3/3 test; outside G330
   dev 3/6, test 10/21), and decisions flipped between gates (weakness-6). None of this can change the test numbers
   now; improvements must be evaluated on dev as exploratory work.

## Findings that touch test results (exactly how)

No test number changes. These change how the test numbers may be **read or quoted**:
- **metrics-time-1:** category mAP@10/@50, instance recall@10/@50 and R@1/5/10 are not comparable across systems
  (60 s baseline segments vs Rewind's median 10-12 s clips; on dev, chance AP@50 is ~6× higher for 60 s units).
  Affected cells: test_results.md Summary (category and R@k columns), category-by-qtype table, RGB-vs-IR R@10 columns.
  Seconds watched is the only unit-free comparison.
- **metrics-time-2 (minor after verification):** the miss rule charges Rewind the full 16.67 h while the sequential
  baseline's expected cost is about half the footage; this drives the "mean" comparisons (Rewind 28,277 vs sequential
  30,282). Defined and documented, but state it next to any mean.
- **metrics-time-3:** 6 of the 13 test hits have no retrieved vector inside the event (found via neighbouring frames
  + ±5 s context): allowed by the frozen hit rule, but "found" ≠ "a matched frame showed the event".
- **claims-1:** secondary vs primary: of 13 events both find, 12 rank lower and **tes-s11 ranks higher**; 8 of the
  "20 slower" are misses by both (list length only). With the gate's both-miss-as-tie convention the paired median is
  about −291 s and its CI includes 0 (vs the committed −352 [−706, −270]).
- **leakage-1/-2:** if the prepared sensitivity scenarios were run as documented, Rewind's test median would move
  1,173 → 409 s, mean 28,277 → 20,650 s, found 13 → 16 of 24 — one-sided (misses → hits only, baselines unchanged), and
  tes-s04 (another day) would cost 0 s. Do not run or quote it as written.

### 1. metrics-time-1 — Category mAP/instance recall and R@k compare Rewind's ~10-15 s clips against baselines' 60 s segments; the comparison favours the baselines, and 'category: no better than sequential or motion' is unsupported

- **Severity:** major (verifier: confirmed; reviewer had major) · **Category:** weakness · **Dimension:** Metrics and timestamps · **Confidence:** high
- **Evidence:** Baselines rank 60 s segments (config.yaml:90, comment 'Rewind clips are capped at 60 s + context'), but Rewind's actual clips are much shorter. In committed test_clips.json the top-10 category clips have mean 12.6 s and median 10 s (n = 100), and specific clips mean 15.5 s and median 12 s (n = 240). The metrics count units, not seconds: AP@k precision is per ranked unit, instance recall@k counts instances overlapped by the top k units, and R@k is a hit in the top k units. A longer unit overlaps an instance by chance much more often. My dev-only check (scratchpad review_pre10/metrics_time/unit_bias.py, the 5 dev category queries, RGB, don't-care removed): P(unit overlaps an instance) is 0.192 for 60 s segments vs 0.059 for 12 s windows, and chance AP@50 (random order) is 0.056 vs 0.009, about 6x. On test the baselines' top 10 therefore cover 600 s of footage against about 155 s for Rewind (e.g. witness R@10 0.090 vs Rewind 0.042), and the top 50 cover 3,000 s against about 630 s. PROGRESS.md:2636 concludes 'Category queries: no better than sequential or motion (mAP@50 0.140 vs 0.174 / 0.158)'. PROGRESS.md:794 noticed that baseline mAP is high because 60 s segments contain events, but did not adjust for it.
- **Affects reported numbers:** All values are computed correctly by their definitions, but the cross-system comparisons in test_results.md (Summary category mAP@10/@50, instance recall@10/@50, R@1/5/10 columns; category-by-qtype table; RGB-vs-IR R@10 columns), baselines_2.3.md / baselines_3.2.md, and the PROGRESS 8.2 reading are biased in the baselines' favour. Gate and ablation decisions compared Rewind variants with each other (same unit), so they are not affected.
- **Affects test numbers:** No test number is wrong.
