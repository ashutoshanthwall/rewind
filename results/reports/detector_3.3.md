- The IR ground truth is not usable at IoU 0.5: 231 of 242 IR boxes are CMU-v2 boxes, known to be out of time and loose. Viewed by Claude (n = 3 IR frames): the detector does box the car, but the CMU box is ~4x larger.
- "No detector" means motion crops. The motion-box baseline on the same boxes is lower on every RGB cell:
  - RGB person 0.101 (YOLOX-s 1280: 0.407);
  - RGB vehicle 0.419 (0.830);
  - mean of cells 0.201.
- Motion's IR centre-in-box value (0.98) is not comparable: motion blobs are large, so their centres fall inside almost any box.
- Recall is low mainly on tiny objects. 62 % of RGB person boxes are < 5 % of the frame height; there YOLOX-s 1280 reaches 0.24, while objects ≥ 15 % of the frame height are found 83-97 %.
